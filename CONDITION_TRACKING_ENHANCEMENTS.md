## Phase 2: Quality Management System

### 2.1 Advanced Quality Grading
- **A+ Grade**: Mint condition, like new
- **A Grade**: Excellent, minor wear if any
- **B Grade**: Good, noticeable but not major wear
- **C Grade**: Fair, functional but significant wear
- **D Grade**: Poor, major issues present
- **F Grade**: Damaged, needs repair or parts

### 2.2 Issue Categorization
- **Cosmetic Issues**: Scratches, dents, discoloration
- **Functional Issues**: Buttons not working, ports damaged
- **Performance Issues**: Slow, battery drains fast
- **Display Issues**: Dead pixels, cracks, backlight problems
- **Audio Issues**: Speaker/microphone problems
- **Hardware Issues**: Bent frame, water damage, component failure
